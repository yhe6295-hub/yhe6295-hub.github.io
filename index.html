OUR SOLAR SYSTEM

Discover the Sun, the planets, and our cosmic home.

The Sun

The Sun is the center of our solar system. It is a star that provides the light and heat needed for life on Earth.

The Sun is a yellow dwarf star. It contains about 99.86% of the total mass of the solar system.


How the Solar System Formed

Our solar system formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a giant cloud of gas and dust.

Milky Way

Gravity pulled gas and dust together. Over time, the Sun formed at the center, while the remaining material formed the planets.
