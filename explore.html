Understanding Our Planets

The eight planets orbit the Sun in a specific order. They have different sizes, compositions and environments. The table below compares their types and positions.

Planet Type Position from the Sun
Mercury Rocky 1st
Venus Rocky 2nd
Earth Rocky 3rd
Mars Rocky 4th
Jupiter Gas Giant 5th
Saturn Gas Giant 6th
Uranus Ice Giant 7th
Neptune Ice Giant 8th

Beyond the Planets

Our solar system contains much more than planets. Moons, asteroids and comets also make it a fascinating place to explore.

There Is More to Discover

Each object in the solar system has its own characteristics. Studying these objects helps us understand how our cosmic neighborhood formed and how it continues to change.
